Enterprise Privacy & Data Governance

Last Updated: November 18, 2026 • Veccna AI Neural Security Protocol

1. Metadata-First Architecture (Zero Payload Ingestion)

Veccna AI operates exclusively on encrypted network metadata, behavioral flow attributes, identity authentication tokens, and cloud audit logs. Under no circumstances does the Veccna Synapse-X neural model decrypt or inspect sensitive customer application payloads, user personal data, or confidential business documents.

The proprietary behavioral transformer models were architected and trained by Amay Vikram Singh, Anshuman, Ankit, and Aaryan using anonymized adversary telemetry, synthetic zero-day honeypot feeds, and public MITRE Caldera emulation datasets. 3. Compliance & Sovereign Isolation

Veccna AI complies with SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, and FedRAMP High baseline requirements. All telemetry storage remains within regional sovereign boundaries (e.g., US-East, EU-Frankfurt) with AES-256-GCM encryption at rest.
